Build a rhythm that works for you

The best routine is the one that meets you where you are.

Start with one moment in your day that already has a natural pause. Take a breath, write down what matters next, and begin. It doesn’t need to be a grand gesture; the point is to make it easy enough to return to tomorrow.

Over time, those small moments add up to a rhythm that feels like yours. Borrow ideas from others, keep what helps, and let the rest go.
